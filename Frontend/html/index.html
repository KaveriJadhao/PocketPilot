Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with PocketPilot.

Is PocketPilot completely free to use?
Yes! PocketPilot is 100% free for college and university students. There are no subscriptions, no credit card requirements, and no hidden paywalls.
How does the Voice AI logging work?
PocketPilot uses your browser's built-in speech recognition engine. When you tap the microphone and say something like "Spent 150 for canteen lunch", our NLP parser extracts the price (₹150), title (Canteen Lunch), and category (Food) in real-time.
Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?
Yes! PocketPilot is 100% responsive and optimized for all mobile screen sizes (from 320px up to 4K displays). You can easily use it on your iPhone, Android phone, iPad, or laptop.
Is my financial data private and secure?
Absolutely. Your password is encrypted with bcrypt hashing, and your account data is protected with secure JWT authentication tokens. PocketPilot never sells or shares your personal information.
